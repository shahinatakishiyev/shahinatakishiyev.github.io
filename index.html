Shahin Atakishiyev
Deep Learning Researcher, Huawei Technologies Canada
University of Alberta Office: UCOMM 6-246B

Shahin Atakishiyev is from the Mirzabayli village of Gabala, Azerbaijan. He received a PhD in Artificial Intelligence in September 2024 and an MSc in Software Engineering and Intelligent Systems in January 2018 from the University of Alberta. He obtained a BSc in Computer Engineering from Qafqaz University, Azerbaijan, in June 2015. During doctoral study, Shahin developed explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) approaches for autonomous vehicles under the supervision of Prof. Randy Goebel and was a member of the XAI lab within the Alberta Machine Intelligence Institute (Amii). His doctoral thesis received the 2024 Outstanding PhD Thesis Runner-up Award in the Department of Computing Science. Shahin's general research interests focus on developing safe and trustworthy AI approaches for real-world problems.

Research Interests
  • Explainable AI, Computer Vision, Foundation Models, Autonomous Driving
In general, research interests focus on developing safe, trustworthy, ethical, and explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) approaches for real-world problems. Current research is centered on applying XAI and Foundation Models to autonomous driving and various downstream computer vision tasks.
